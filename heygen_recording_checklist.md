# HeyGen Digital Twin – Recording Checklist

Source: HeyGen help center filming tips, Avatar IV guide, and developer docs (Oct 2026).

## Length
- [ ] 2–5 minutes, one continuous take (aim for ~3). No cuts, no edits, no splicing.
- [ ] If you flub a line, keep talking naturally or restart from the beginning.

## Camera & framing
- [ ] At least 1080p, 30fps (4K is fine; 60fps adds nothing).
- [ ] Camera on a tripod/stable surface at eye level. Don't touch it once recording.
- [ ] Head and upper torso in frame, face centered, face ~50% of frame height.
- [ ] Leave room above your head and keep centered (so ads can be cropped to vertical 9:16).
- [ ] Stay the same distance from the camera the whole time.

## Lighting
- [ ] Bright, even light on your face; no harsh shadows, no blown-out highlights.
- [ ] Light in front of you (ring light or two lights at ~45°), not behind you.
- [ ] Avoid window light that can change during the take.
- [ ] Phone: tap-and-hold your face to lock focus/exposure.

## Audio
- [ ] Quiet room: no voices, fans, HVAC, music, or echo.
- [ ] Your voice is the loudest thing the mic hears (external mic or phone close by is better than laptop mic).
- [ ] Audio must be in the file – no audio = rejected.

## Outfit
- [ ] Solid colors; no big logos, text, stripes, or busy patterns.
- [ ] Wear what you want to appear in the ads (e.g., clinic polo without a large logo).
- [ ] Nothing that covers your face or mouth.

## Background
- [ ] Plain and static (solid wall, or a clean, uncluttered clinic corner).
- [ ] Nothing moving behind you (people, screens, doors).

## During the recording
- [ ] Start with 2–3 seconds of silence, mouth closed, relaxed smile.
- [ ] Look directly into the lens the whole time (teleprompter right under the lens if used).
- [ ] Talk naturally and conversationally, like explaining something to a patient.
- [ ] Pause 1–2 seconds between longer sentences with lips closed.
- [ ] Keep expressions alive during pauses (small smile, nods).
- [ ] Moderate hand gestures, below chest, inside the frame, then return to a resting position.
- [ ] No symbolic gestures (thumbs up, pointing, counting on fingers) – the twin will repeat them.
- [ ] Head turns under ~30°; no sudden movements.
- [ ] Do 1–2 warm-up takes before the real one.

## Consent video (Step 5)
- [ ] Recorded at the same time, same person, same setup; under 30 seconds. HeyGen gives you the script.
