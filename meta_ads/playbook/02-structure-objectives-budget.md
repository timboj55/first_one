## 1. Principles

1. **Keep the structure simple.** Most small businesses need one cold campaign and, later, one retargeting campaign. "I would just actually start thinking about top of funnel, cold outreach and retargeting. That'll be enough for most of you" (MC "TOF, MOF, BOF: Funnel Explained" [3:28]). The lesson text promises "Why simpler structures perform better" (MC "Facebook Ads Structure Explained", lesson text).
2. **The objective is make-or-break.** "Even if you get ... everything else, absolutely 100% spot on. If you don't do this bit right, your ads will fail" (MC "Objectives: What's the Difference?" [0:00]). Use Leads for services, Sales for e-commerce. "These two are the ones that you will stick by" (MC "Objectives" [8:26]).
3. **Awareness and Traffic do not get action for a small business.** "An awareness campaign will not get people to your website taking action ... Traffic ... they'll leave straight away ... So it's a waste of money" (MC "TOF, MOF, BOF" [1:43]).
4. **Use the manual setup for control.** "I like to stick to manual leads campaign just because it gives us more options of more control" (LG "Setting up a Meta Lead Form campaign" [1:24]).
5. **Small budgets are fine.** "You're absolutely fine doing 10 to 20 a day. Five is a little bit too little" (LWU "Budget Basics: Spend Smart" [3:34]). Most businesses "don't necessarily need to spend stupid amounts of daily spend" (TEST "Scaling rules" [0:48]).
6. **Scale slowly. Big budget jumps reset learning.** "A scale really is not going up more than 15% of your daily budget every three, two to three days" (TEST "Scaling rules" [2:39]).
7. **Local audiences saturate.** "Being local, you just need to think about, you don't want to go too high and saturate your audience too quickly" (LOCAL "Setting Up Your Local Lead Campaign" [0:49]; same transcript in LG "Building a Local Campaign" [0:49]).
8. **Don't fear the learning phase.** "Most of my clients live in learning phase and still get really good results" (LWU "Budget Basics" [0:53]).
9. **Scale only as fast as you can handle the leads.** If he turned all budgets up, "I would be inundated ... we have members of staff who can deal with those calls, but I have to create that structure" (TEST "Scaling rules" [1:44]).

## 2. Rules

### Structure (R-STR)

### R-STR-01: Know what each level controls
- When: Building or reading any campaign.
- Do: Set things at the right level.
  - **Campaign:** objective, and budget if you use campaign budget.
  - **Ad set:** conversion location (instant form or website), performance goal, dataset/event, budget (if ad set budget), schedule, location/radius, age, audience, placements.
  - **Ad:** identity (Page/Instagram), format, creative, primary text, headline, CTA, destination form or URL, tracking.
- Don't: Hunt for audience or budget settings at ad level, or creative at ad set level.
- Source: MC "Facebook Ads Structure Explained" and "Ad Hierarchy" (lesson text only: "where to set your goals, budgets, audiences, and creative"); MC "Build an Lead Gen Ad" [0:51]-[4:19]; LWU "Budget Basics" [4:24]. The lesson text says the video covers "Where USP testing really belongs", but there is no transcript, so the course's answer is unknown here.

### R-STR-02: Use the manual Leads campaign setup
- When: Creating any lead campaign.
- Do: Create > Leads > **Manual leads campaign**.
- Don't: Use the "tailored" / Advantage+ leads setup. It "knocks out the ad set side", so you lose ad set control.
- Source: LG "Setting up a Meta Lead Form campaign" [0:00]-[1:24]; LG "Setting up a Meta Landing page campaign" [0:56]; LWU "Budget Basics" [4:24] ("Manual leads as always").

### R-STR-03: Start with one campaign, one ad set, a few ads
- When: New account, new offer, or budget under about £/$50 a day.
- Do: Run one cold (TOF) Leads campaign with one ad set and a small number of ads. To test something new, duplicate the ad set and change one thing. Add a retargeting campaign only once you have an audience to retarget (R-STR-08).
- Don't: Build a full TOF/MOF/BOF funnel up front. "I don't think you need to go that deep into it" (ECOM "Typical eCom funnel structure" [12:04]). Full-funnel structures are for big budgets or high site activity.
- Numbers: The low-budget lesson covers £10–£20/day. A worked example ran "one campaign and inside a campaign, we've got our three ad sets" at £20/day.
- Source: MC "TOF, MOF, BOF" [3:28]; MC "Why you don't always need TOF-MOF-BOF" (lesson text); LWU "Breaking Down a Winning Ad" [1:48], [6:36]; LWU "The NEW way to run Ads on a low budget" (lesson text); LG "Importance of lead magnets, forms, booking flows" [0:00] (don't build the whole funnel up front).

### R-STR-04: Set the destination at ad set level and keep one destination per ad set
- When: Choosing between an instant form and a landing page.
- Do: Pick the conversion location in the ad set. To compare destinations, run an instant-form ad set and a landing-page ad set side by side in the same campaign. Meta defaults to Instant forms. Check the right Facebook Page and Instagram account are selected.
- Don't: Use "Website and instant forms" (Meta picks per user) as a default. He calls it "another great add on" but rarely uses it.
- Source: LG "Setting up a Meta Landing page campaign" [1:45], [4:24]-[5:12]; LOCAL "Setting Up Your Local Lead Campaign" [0:49]; LOCAL "Visual is what stops the scroll!" [2:11] (forms give "more results at a lower cost" for local service ads).
- Clinic: **[Clinic adaptation]** Default every clinic ad set to **Instant forms**. Website-pixel lead optimization may not be available to a health advertiser and the pixel carries HIPAA risk. A landing-page ad set is a test only, run on non-condition URLs, with Meta's restriction checked first. See CL-RES-01 and CL-HIP-01.

### R-STR-05: Above about £50/day, run a testing campaign alongside a master campaign
- When: Spend is roughly £/$50+ a day and you are testing creative regularly.
- Do:
  - Keep a **master (control) campaign** that holds proven winners.
  - Run tests in a separate **testing campaign** (or, at lower spend, as new ad sets in the one campaign).
  - Move a winner into the master campaign with **Use existing post**, so likes and comments carry over. Steps: in the test campaign, select the winning ad > Preview > preview settings > "Facebook post with comments" > copy the post ID (the number at the end of the URL). In the master campaign's new ad > Ad setup > switch "Create ad" to "Use existing post" > Enter post ID > paste > tick. Only the CTA and URL/lead form can be changed.
- Don't: Rebuild a winner from scratch in the master campaign. You lose its social proof.
- Numbers: £50+/day (lesson title).
- Source: LWU "£50+ a day? The BEST way to advertise" (lesson text only: "structure your testing and control campaigns"); TEST "Move WINNING Test Ad into Master Campaign" [0:00] and lesson text ("this keeps all previous ad social proof!"). The course gives no transcript-level detail on how the two campaigns are budgeted against each other.
- Clinic: **[Clinic adaptation]** At the clinic's likely local budget, stay with one campaign plus new test ad sets (R-STR-03) until spend passes about $50/day. Carried-over comments may include people mentioning conditions. Moderate them and never reply in a way that confirms someone is a patient (general best practice, not from course). Size the master and test campaigns to the radius audience, not to the course's national examples. See CL-LOC-02.

### R-STR-06: Post-Andromeda, adapt testing rather than rebuilding the account
- When: Meta delivery changes (the "Andromeda" update) and results shift.
- Do: Keep the account structure. Go broad on audience. Put the effort into creative diversification (UGC, testimonials, demos, offers, benefit-led ads) and testing concepts.
- Don't: "panic or rebuild everything overnight".
- Source: LWU "PROVEN campaign structure, that works!" (lesson text); LWU "The NEW way to run Ads on a low budget" (lesson text); LWU "£50+ a day?" (lesson text). **The course is thin here.** All three are untranscribed videos. The only detail is in the lesson blurbs.
- Clinic: **[Clinic adaptation]** Testimonials need written HIPAA authorization (CL-HIP-05). Creative call-outs must not assert a condition (CL-POL-01).

### R-STR-07: Use Advantage+ for audience and placements, not for the campaign setup or creative
- When: Setting up any lead ad set and ad.
- Do:
  - **Advantage+ audience:** on, broad, no interests. Add only an age suggestion if you know the buyer's age.
  - **Advantage+ placements:** on (Facebook and Instagram, all placements).
  - **Advantage+ creative enhancements:** off at ad level ("They make the ads look scruffy").
  - **Tailored / Advantage+ leads campaign:** not used (R-STR-02).
  - **Advantage campaign budget:** see R-BUD-04.
  - **Dynamic creative / Flexible format:** optional on small budgets (detail in the creative and testing files).
  - Leave buying type on Auction. Leave bid strategy at the default. Don't set campaign spending limits.
- Don't: Set a cost-per-result goal (cost cap). A cap below what is achievable starves delivery.
- Source: LG "Setting up a Meta Lead Form campaign" [2:19], [3:47], [8:02], [16:21]-[17:23]; LG "Setting up a Meta Landing page campaign" [3:37], [9:29]; LWU "Breaking Down a Winning Ad" [3:58]; ECOM "Catalog Sales vs Manual Campaigns" [9:42] (no cost cap); MC "Build an Ecomm Ad" [11:10]-[12:27].
- Clinic: **[Clinic adaptation]** "Broad" for the clinic means broad inside the local radius. Don't add condition interests (largely removed by Meta, see CL-POL-01) and don't upload patient lists (CL-HIP-02). Health/PT is not a Special Ad Category, so don't declare one (LG "Setting up a Meta Lead Form campaign" [2:19] lists the categories).

### R-STR-08: Run retargeting as its own small campaign, on the same Leads objective
- When: The cold campaign has built up engaged people (video viewers, form openers, page/IG engagers).
- Do: Create a separate retargeting campaign, still Leads, manual, with a reminder-style creative ("you were on the fence"). In larger accounts, use a "7-day stack" of recent engagers with about 5 ads.
- Don't: Use Awareness or Traffic for retargeting.
- Numbers: Budget in R-BUD-06.
- Source: MC "TOF, MOF, BOF" [11:04]-[12:38] ("I'm going to keep it at Leeds [Leads] because we still want them to become a lead"); TEST "How small wins compound over time" [9:06]-[10:20]; ECOM "Typical eCom funnel structure" [9:29]-[10:22].
- Clinic: **[Clinic adaptation]** The course's example retargets website visitors with website conversion optimization. For the clinic, build retargeting from on-platform engagement (video viewers, lead-form openers, page/IG engagers) and send it to an instant form. Website audiences only from generic pages. See CL-RES-01 and CL-HIP-06.

### R-STR-09: Name every level so you can read results without opening anything
- When: Every new campaign, ad set and ad.
- Do:
  - Campaign = funnel stage + objective + destination/area, e.g. "TOF - Lead - Instant form - [Town]".
  - Ad set = the audience actually set + radius + test + date, e.g. "Advantage+ | 40mi | Hook test | 18 Jul 25".
  - Ad = creative/hook name, matching the file name in your asset folder.
  - Remove the automatic " - Copy" suffix after duplicating.
- Source: LG "Setting up a Meta Lead Form campaign" [1:24]; LOCAL "Setting Up Your Local Lead Campaign" [0:00], [2:26], [4:04]; LWU "Campaign Naming Tips" [0:00]-[2:41]; TEST "How to structure a basic testing campaign" [0:54].
- Clinic: **[Clinic adaptation]** Keep condition names out of campaign, ad set, ad, form and URL-parameter names where they could flow into tracking. Use offer or creative names. See CL-HIP-01.

### Objective (R-OBJ)

### R-OBJ-01: Use the Leads objective for the clinic
- When: Any campaign meant to produce patients.
- Do: Choose **Leads** for cold and retargeting campaigns.
- Don't: Use Awareness, Traffic or Engagement to get leads. Traffic attracts "bots ... clickers who go to your website and spend a millisecond there" (MC "Objectives" [5:04]). Sales is the e-commerce objective (e-commerce; not applicable to the clinic).
- Source: MC "Objectives" [0:00]-[8:26]; MC "TOF, MOF, BOF" [1:43]; LG "Awareness > Value > Trust > Action" [3:18] ("lead objective, lead objective"); MC "Why you don't always need TOF-MOF-BOF" (lesson text).
- Clinic: **[Clinic adaptation]** Leads with **instant forms** is the default. Several course builds (MC "Build an Lead Gen Ad", LG landing-page lesson) assume Leads optimized on a website pixel event. Those events may be unavailable to a health advertiser. See CL-RES-01.

### R-OBJ-02: Optimize for leads, never for link clicks or landing page views
- When: Setting the ad set performance goal.
- Do: Set the goal to "Maximize number of leads" (instant form) or "Maximize number of conversions" on the Lead event (website). Check it every time. Meta sometimes defaults to link clicks.
- Don't: Choose link clicks or landing page views. That effectively makes it a Traffic campaign "and your results are going to be awful".
- Source: LG "Setting up a Meta Landing page campaign" [5:12]; ECOM "Catalog Sales vs Manual Campaigns" [7:52]-[8:44] ("massive red flag").
- Clinic: **[Clinic adaptation]** If the clinic's website Lead event is restricted, don't fall back to landing page views on a website ad set. Use instant forms, where the lead is counted on Meta. See CL-RES-01.

### R-OBJ-03: Don't "bypass" Meta's health-data restriction
- When: Ads Manager shows no website conversions for a health advertiser.
- Do: Accept it as a category-level restriction. The course itself says changing copy or creative won't fix it (LWU "Meta Restricts Health Business Tracking", lesson text).
- Don't: Adopt the course's promised "tracking solution we're using to bypass these restrictions" or any tool that feeds conversion data back to Meta around the restriction. The lesson never names the mechanism.
- Source: LWU "Meta Restricts Health Business Tracking (Here's How We Bypass It)" (lesson text only).
- Clinic: **[Clinic adaptation]** Do not follow for the clinic. Use instant-form Leads and measure bookings outside Meta. See CL-RES-02, CL-RES-03 and CL-KPI-03.

### Budget (R-BUD)

### R-BUD-01: Run on a daily budget with no end date
- When: Setting budget and schedule on any ad set or campaign.
- Do: Use a **daily** budget and leave the end date blank. Check the account daily instead.
- Don't: Use a lifetime budget or set an end date. An end date can switch off a winner without anyone noticing.
- Source: LG "Setting up a Meta Lead Form campaign" [5:35]; LG "Setting up a Meta Landing page campaign" [1:45], [6:59].

### R-BUD-02: Size starting budgets small
- When: Launching a campaign or test.
- Do: Start with a modest daily budget per ad set and add ad sets only when you accept the extra spend.
- Numbers (as the course states):
  - Normal working budget £/$10–20/day. "Five is a little bit too little" for prospecting (LWU "Budget Basics" [3:34]).
  - Cold/TOF starter about 20/day (MC "TOF, MOF, BOF" [12:38]); national instant-form example $20/day (LG "Setting up a Meta Lead Form campaign" [5:35]).
  - Local campaign £5–10/day per ad set, to avoid saturating a small audience (LOCAL "Setting Up Your Local Lead Campaign" [0:49]).
  - Test ad set about £30/day. A second audience-test ad set doubles spend to £60/day (TEST "What to test" [6:54], [12:54]).
  - Flexible format under £50/day: use 3–5 creatives, not 10 (LWU "Building a Flexible Ad" [0:48]).
- Don't: Pour money in before the path from ad to booked call is proven. Spend "the right amount ... to get your learnings" (ECOM "Understanding product/market fit" [1:09]).
- Source: as listed under Numbers.
- Clinic: **[Clinic adaptation]** Size the budget to the clinic's radius audience and appointment capacity, not to the course's national examples. Start near the local figure and raise only when frequency and cost per booked evaluation hold. See CL-LOC-02 and CL-KPI-01.

### R-BUD-03: Treat Meta's learning-phase budget formula as a guide, not a target
- When: Deciding whether a budget is "enough".
- Do: Know the formula: daily budget = (50 × current cost per result) ÷ 7. Then judge on results, not the "Learning" label. Tolerate "Learning limited" while cost per result is on target. Check every couple of days.
- Don't: Raise budget just to exit learning. Exiting learning stabilises cost; it doesn't dramatically lower it.
- Numbers: Meta wants 50 conversions in 7 days. Examples: 50 × £4.90 = £245 ÷ 7 = £35/day (TEST "Scaling rules" [5:52]-[6:44]); 50 × £4 = £200 ÷ 7 ≈ £28/day, which exited learning on 23 conversions; 50 × £21 = £1,050 ÷ 7 = £150/day, which exited learning on 10 leads (LWU "Budget Basics" [1:47]-[3:34]).
- Source: TEST "Scaling rules" [5:52]-[6:44]; TEST "How small wins compound over time" [7:48]-[9:06] ("I do take them with a pinch of salt"); LWU "Budget Basics" [0:53]-[3:34].
- Clinic: **[Clinic adaptation]** A local clinic will rarely reach 50 leads a week per ad set. Expect to live in learning, and judge by cost per booked evaluation and show rate rather than learning status or CPL alone. See CL-KPI-01 and CL-LOC-02.

### R-BUD-04: Use ad set budgets (ABO) when testing ad sets; it doesn't matter with one ad set
- When: Choosing between campaign budget (Advantage campaign budget, CBO) and ad set budget (ABO).
- Do:
  - One ad set: either is fine. "If you're just having one ad set, it doesn't really matter at all."
  - Several ad sets you want to compare (e.g. two audiences, a hook test): turn Advantage campaign budget **off** and give each ad set an equal budget, then move budget by hand to the winner (e.g. keep £10 on one, raise the other to £30).
  - Let Meta allocate (CBO) only when you are happy for it to push most spend to whichever ad set it prefers.
- Don't: Expect an even split under CBO. Meta pushes "most of the budget" to the ad set it thinks will win.
- Numbers: £20 split as £10 + £10; winner raised to £30, then £40.
- Source: LG "Setting up a Meta Lead Form campaign" [2:19] (ad set budget "keeps me again in more control ... a complete change to what I was doing just over a year ago"); LWU "Budget Basics" [5:14]-[8:35]; LOCAL "Setting Up Your Local Lead Campaign" [0:00]; ECOM "Catalog Sales vs Manual Campaigns" [5:11] (keeps CBO for e-commerce). Bug note: the ad set budget may not save first time; re-enter it (LWU "Budget Basics" [7:46]).

### R-BUD-05: Don't use budget scheduling or switch ads off at weekends
- When: Leads come mostly in the evening or on weekdays, and you are tempted to daypart.
- Do: Leave the daily budget steady and let Meta place spend. "Meta will spend less in the daytime if it knows that people are filling in your lead form in the evening."
- Don't: Use Meta's budget scheduling on new campaigns. Don't turn ads off over the weekend and back on Monday ("it really messes with the optimisation").
- Numbers: The tool allows increases by value or percentage per date range, up to 50 periods, dates only (LWU example: £15 base, +£10 Mon 7am to Fri 7pm).
- Source: LG "Setting up a Meta Lead Form campaign" [5:35], [8:02]; LG "Setting up a Meta Landing page campaign" [7:52]-[8:41] ("I would stay clear of this"); LWU "How to set up Budget Scheduling" (lesson text: "Avoid it for new campaigns") and [0:00]-[2:05].
- Note: LWU says budget scheduling is worth a try once you have clear day-of-week data. They run it on "a couple" of accounts but have "not seen enough shift in performance" to roll it out. This playbook defaults to off (see section 3).

### R-BUD-06: Keep the retargeting budget small
- When: Setting the retargeting campaign's budget.
- Do: Run retargeting at a fraction of cold spend, about £/$5 a day while the audience builds. Raise it only as the warm audience grows and frequency allows.
- Don't: Accept Meta's auto-filled budget (20/day in the demo) for a small retargeting pool. Frequency climbs and it stops working.
- Numbers: about £5/day ("like a fiver", MC "TOF, MOF, BOF" [12:38]; LWU "Budget Basics" [3:34]). In a large clinic account spending about $4,000–4,500/week on TOF, the middle-of-funnel 7-day stack ran at $100/day (TEST "How small wins compound over time" [0:57], [10:20]).
- Source: as listed.
- Clinic: **[Clinic adaptation]** The clinic's warm pool will be small. Watch retargeting frequency weekly and keep this budget low. See CL-LOC-01 and CL-LOC-02.

### R-BUD-07: Match spend to the team's capacity to follow up and to calendar availability
- When: Before any budget increase.
- Do: Raise spend only if staff can contact new leads fast and the calendar has near-term slots. Cut spend when bookings land too far out, because people forget and no-show.
- Numbers: Example cut spend about 50% (about 250 to 100 a day) once the calendar was full (LWU "Unlock Facebook's Hidden Ad Feature" [0:00]).
- Source: TEST "Scaling rules" [0:48]-[1:44]; LWU "Unlock Facebook's Hidden Ad Feature" [0:00].
- Clinic: **[Clinic adaptation]** Tie budget to front-desk response time and open evaluation slots in IntakeQ. See CL-KPI-02.

### Scaling (R-SCL)

### R-SCL-01: Scale only when results hold
- When: An ad or ad set has run for a few days at or under target.
- Do: Scale when cost per result is on target and the downstream numbers "stack up". Judge leads by booking, show and close rates as well as CPL. "A higher CPL can be fine if leads close."
- Don't: Scale on a couple of good days or on CTR/CPC alone.
- Source: ECOM "Typical eCom funnel structure" [8:35]; LG "What does success look like?" [1:30]; LG "Key Metrics to Watch" [6:48]-[7:39]; TEST "What to test" [6:54], [11:09] (review after a few days or "after the weekend").
- Clinic: **[Clinic adaptation]** The scaling signal is cost per booked evaluation and show rate from the weekly GoHighLevel/IntakeQ reconciliation, not Meta CPL. See CL-KPI-01 and CL-KPI-03.

### R-SCL-02: Scale vertically in small steps
- When: Raising a winning ad set's daily budget.
- Do: Raise by no more than about 15% every 2–3 days (the course gives 10–20% in different lessons). Lowering by large amounts also risks learning.
- Don't: Jump 3x–10x. "You've changed everything" and it goes back into learning.
- Numbers: ≤15% every 2–3 days (TEST "Scaling rules" [2:39]). "15 to 20%" (LG "Setting up a Meta Lead Form campaign" [5:35]). "Meta says 20% ... I think actually 10 to 15%", with £10 → £12 fine (LG "Setting up a Meta Landing page campaign" [7:52]).
- Source: as listed; TEST "Scaling rules" [6:44]-[7:40].
- Clinic: **[Clinic adaptation]** Use the low end (10–15%) and wait a full week between steps if frequency is rising. A radius audience runs out faster than the national audiences in the course. See CL-LOC-02.

### R-SCL-03: Scale horizontally by duplicating, knowing it restarts learning
- When: You want more spend on a winner faster than vertical steps allow.
- Do: Duplicate the winning ad set, keep only the winning ad, and give it a higher budget or a different audience. Name it with the date.
- Don't: Expect the duplicate to match the original. It "starts again" in learning, so results are unpredictable.
- Source: TEST "Scaling rules" [3:26]-[7:40]; LG "Setting up a Meta Lead Form campaign" [5:35] ("duplicate the ad and put a higher spend on it and hope that it captures the same").
- Clinic: **[Clinic adaptation]** In one radius, a duplicate competes for the same small audience. Prefer slow vertical steps and new creative tests. See CL-LOC-02.

### R-SCL-04: Scale by adding creative, not just budget
- When: Growing an account over weeks and months.
- Do:
  - Keep running test ad sets of new hooks and iterations next to the winners.
  - Give a strong winning ad its own ad set so it gets its own budget.
  - Turn off ad sets running at "double ... if not treble" the target CPA once a new one matches or beats them.
  - Keep a winning test ad running. "Because it's a test doesn't mean you have to switch it off."
- Numbers: A 40-mile-radius US clinic grew from "a few ads at like $20 a day" to about $4,000–4,500/week, about $18,000/month, at a CPL target under $30 (TEST "How small wins compound over time" [0:57], [11:37]).
- Source: TEST "When to test again" [0:51]-[3:23]; TEST "One variable at a time" [5:59]; TEST "How small wins compound over time" [4:56]-[7:48]; LG "Key Metrics to Watch" [5:03]-[5:57].
- Clinic: **[Clinic adaptation]** Creative volume is how a radius account scales. Keep a creative bank and refresh on fatigue. Judge kills by cost per booked evaluation where you have it, not CPL alone. See CL-LOC-01 and CL-KPI-01.

### R-SCL-05: Watch frequency as you scale in a small area
- When: Any budget increase, and weekly.
- Do: Rotate out or turn off an ad once frequency passes 2. Treat a climb toward 3 as a sign that costs are about to rise and fresh creative is needed. In a small radius, answer saturation with new creatives, hooks and landing pages, not by changing the audience.
- Numbers: frequency > 2 → turned off (TEST "Scaling rules" [8:33]); "we don't want our frequency to go to [three]" (LWU "Paid vs Organic" [3:07]-[4:35]). A 40-mile functional medicine clinic spent about $2,000/week (MC "Inside the Meta Ecosystem" [8:32]-[9:43]).
- Source: as listed.
- Clinic: **[Clinic adaptation]** See CL-LOC-01 (weekly frequency and fatigue checks) and CL-LOC-02 (size budget to the local audience).

## 3. Open questions / contradictions in the course

1. **CBO vs ABO.**
   - LG says ad set budget for control, "a complete change to what I was doing just over a year ago".
   - ECOM keeps campaign budget "as Meta's telling us".
   - LWU "Budget Basics" says it doesn't matter with one ad set, and to turn CBO off when you want an even test.
   - **Default:** ABO for any test of two or more ad sets; either is fine with one ad set (R-BUD-04).
2. **Budget-change step size.**
   - TEST says ≤15% every 2–3 days, but its own demo goes £20 → £25 (25%) and he says jumping 20 → 40 "does all right".
   - LG says "15 to 20%" in one lesson and "Meta says 20% ... I think actually 10 to 15%" in another.
   - **Default:** 10–15% steps for the clinic, because a radius audience is small (R-SCL-02).
3. **Learning phase.**
   - The 50-conversions-in-7-days formula is presented as Meta's rule.
   - LWU shows ad sets exiting learning on 23 and on 10 conversions, and says most clients "live in learning phase".
   - **Default:** know the formula, don't chase it; judge on downstream results (R-BUD-03).
4. **Budget scheduling.**
   - LG: "stay clear of this"; swings push ads back into learning.
   - LWU lesson text: "ideal for scaling spend during high-traffic periods", but "avoid it for new campaigns". The LWU video says results shifted only "slightly".
   - **Default:** off. Revisit only after months of stable data showing a clear weekday pattern (R-BUD-05).
5. **Minimum budget.**
   - LWU: £5/day is "a little bit too little"; £10–20 is fine.
   - LOCAL/LG local lesson: £5–10/day for local ad sets.
   - Both run retargeting at about £5.
   - **Default:** start near the local figure for a single radius, because saturation is the bigger risk for the clinic (R-BUD-02, CL-LOC-02).
6. **Small local audience vs big local spend.**
   - LOCAL says keep budgets small so you don't saturate.
   - TEST and MC show 40-mile-radius clinics at about $2,000/week and about $18,000/month.
   - **Default:** scale is possible in a radius but is driven by creative volume and frequency control, not budget alone (R-SCL-04, R-SCL-05).
7. **Funnel naming.**
   - MC says you don't need MOF, then calls the two-campaign setup "middle funnel, top of funnel, retarget".
   - TEST calls retargeting "middle of funnel".
   - **Default:** two campaigns, named TOF and Retargeting (R-STR-03, R-STR-08).
8. **Advantage+ everywhere?**
   - Advantage+ audience and placements are recommended.
   - Advantage+ creative enhancements and the tailored Advantage+ leads campaign are rejected.
   - The Andromeda lessons (text only) say to "adapt your strategy for Advantage+ performance".
   - **Default:** Advantage+ audience and placements on; manual campaign; enhancements off (R-STR-07).
9. **Pixel "required" for Leads.**
   - MC/LOCAL say you can't use the Leads objective without a dataset/pixel.
   - The same courses build Leads → Instant forms, which needs no pixel.
   - **Default:** instant forms, no pixel dependency (R-STR-04, R-OBJ-01, CL-RES-01).
10. **Instant form vs landing page.**
    - LOCAL: forms give "more results at a lower cost".
    - MC: website is "easiest" and forms "may" help; just "test".
    - **Default:** instant forms for the clinic, for both policy and course reasons.
11. **Objective wording.**
    - LG "Awareness > Value > Trust > Action" says "lead objective" repeatedly, then "We are going to use a sales objective" [sic].
    - **Default:** treat this as a mis-speak; use Leads.
12. **Testing vs master campaign.**
    - The £50+/day lesson and the "Move WINNING Test Ad" lesson assume separate test and master campaigns.
    - TEST "What to test" and LWU "Breaking Down a Winning Ad" run tests as new ad sets inside one campaign.
    - The budget threshold and how to split budget between them are only in untranscribed videos.
    - **Default:** one campaign below about $50/day; split into test and master above it (R-STR-03, R-STR-05).
