## Key metrics: ROAS, AOV, Cost per Purchase

Section: Optimising for Sales

*Video: lesson video hosted in Skool (no transcript available).*

#### Lesson text

If you don’t measure it, you can’t scale it. Learn what ROAS, AOV, and Cost per Purchase *really* tell you — and how to use them to judge performance and drive profitable growth.

High level:  
LP > ATC = 10%  
ATC > IC = 40%  
ATC > Purchase = 60 - 80%  
LP > Purchase 2-4%
