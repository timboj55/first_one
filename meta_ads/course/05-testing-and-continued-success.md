## How to find out your winning ad copy

Section: Step 1: Reading Your Results  
Source: https://www.skool.com/theadsclinic/classroom/b581393a?md=1858d306482741d1a2564a243d740ad6

*Video: lesson video hosted in Skool (no transcript available).*

#### Lesson text

One of the quickest ways to figure out what’s driving results in your ad is to break down your data properly inside Ads Manager (if Meta hasnt removed your function).

Here’s how:

1. Go to Ads Manager → Find the campaign/ad set/ad you want to analyse.
2. Select Breakdown (top right dropdown).
3. Choose “By Dynamic Creative Asset” → “Text” or “Headline”.
4. You’ll now see a performance split by each headline and primary text variation.
5. Look at key metrics like CTR, CPC, and Conversions — not just impressions.

👉 The one with the highest ROI, CTR and best cost per result is your winner.

⚡ Remember: small text tweaks can create big differences in performance.
