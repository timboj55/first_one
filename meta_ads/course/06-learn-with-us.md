## How to set up Budget Scheduling

Section: Utilising Your Budget  
Source: https://www.skool.com/theadsclinic/classroom/8995064f?md=aef952b77166408b834a1860cfb1278d

*Video: lesson video hosted in Skool (no transcript available).*

#### Lesson text

Budget Scheduling can be ideal for scaling spend during high-traffic periods, sales events, or times when your audience is most active, without needing to manually adjust budgets.

Use it when you’ve got data showing clear performance patterns (like stronger weekend sales or weekday conversions). Avoid it for new campaigns as sudden budget shifts can reset performance.

Think of it as giving your ads a smart rhythm, spending more when results are likely to be best, and conserving budget when they’re not.

Give it a go and let us know how you get on!

## Meta Restricts Health Business Tracking

Source: https://www.skool.com/theadsclinic/classroom/8995064f?md=411456e9094c4fca9296396c13040edd

#### Lesson text

**(Here's How We Bypass It)**

If you're running Meta ads for health and wellness businesses, here's what's probably happening:

✅ Your campaigns deliver.

✅ People click.

🛑 But Meta shows zero conversion data.

You can't optimise. Columns stay blank. The algorithm is flying blind.

Here's what most advertisers don't realise -> 🚨This isn't a campaign problem. It's a Meta restriction problem.

When your business falls into the "health and wellness" category, Meta actively restricts how your conversion signals flow back into Ads Manager.

🛑 Weight loss? Blocked.  
🛑 Supplements? Blocked.  
🛑 Hormone clinics? Blocked.  
🛑 Functional medicine? Blocked.  
🛑 And many more....

The standard pixel simply can't send the signals Meta needs to optimise your campaigns.

So you're stuck running ads with one hand tied behind your back.

In this video, I show you:  
✅ Why Meta blocks conversion signals for health & wellness accounts  
✅ Why rewriting copy or changing creatives won't fix this  
✅ The tracking solution we're using to bypass these restrictions  
✅ Full walkthrough showing how to feed real conversion data back into Meta  
✅ This has already unlocked multiple client accounts that were stuck spending money with zero visibility.
